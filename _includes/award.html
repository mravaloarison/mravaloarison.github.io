{%- comment -%} A hackathon award as one unit: the trophy, then the award on the first line and the university, the event and the year on the line below. The university comes first because it's the name people recognise. The trophy is Google's 🏆 (Noto Emoji artwork, assets/emoji/trophy.svg), centred in a soft yellow square as tall as the two lines beside it (the square is the image's padding and background, see .award-icon). alt="" because the award text says it all. On project pages (size="large") it's a box under the abstract. In project lists (size="card") it's lighter, in line with the title beside the small thumbnail: a small trophy (no square) before the award in bold, then university · year under the award, the year tied to the last word with no-break spaces so it never sits alone on a line.
