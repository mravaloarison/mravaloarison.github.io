{%- comment -%} Looks up one Arts subcategory from _data/categories.yml and its pieces in gallery order: the ones with an `order` first (1, 2, 3…), then the rest newest first. Param: slug (hand-drawn | procreate | animated). Sets `group` and `group_items` for the template that includes it. PortfolioSEO.art_group_items (_plugins/seo_helpers.rb) keeps the same order. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign group = site.data.categories.arts | where: "slug", include.slug | first -%} {%- if group.collection == "animations" -%} {%- assign group_all = site.animations | sort: "date" | reverse -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign group_all = site.arts | where: "medium", group.name | sort: "date" | reverse -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign group_ordered = group_all | where_exp: "it", "it.order != nil" | sort: "order" -%} {%- assign group_rest = group_all | where_exp: "it", "it.order == nil" -%} {%- assign group_items = group_ordered | concat: group_rest -%}
